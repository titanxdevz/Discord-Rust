# Client-Facing Quotation Template

Never include internal hourly cost, buffer, fees math, minimum price, tier
logic or negotiation limits. Do not invent client names, addresses or company
details; leave placeholders. Use the client's currency and plain language.

---

**Quotation: [Project title]**
Prepared for: [Client name, if provided] | Date: [today] | Valid until: [date, 7-15 days]

**1. Overview**
Two or three sentences on the goal and what will be delivered.

**2. Deliverables and included features**
Bulleted list of confirmed features only.

**3. Timeline**
Delivery window, and what it depends on (content, credentials, approvals). Clock starts when the advance and required inputs are received.

**4. Price**
Total fixed price: [amount, currency]. [GST/taxes: added separately / not applicable.] Exchange rate (if converted) locked until the validity date.

**5. Payment schedule**
Milestones with percentages and triggers.

**6. Revisions**
[2] rounds of changes within the agreed scope. Work beyond it is quoted separately.

**7. Acceptance**
Work is accepted when the listed deliverables work as described in section 2, or after [5] working days without reported issues.

**8. Exclusions**
Anything not listed above, including: hosting and domain fees, third-party subscriptions and API costs, content writing, stock images and fonts (unless listed), data migration (unless listed), ongoing maintenance.

**9. Warranty and support**
[15-30] days of fixes for defects against the agreed requirements. New features or changes are not warranty work. Maintenance is available separately: [terms or "on request"].

**10. Ownership and licences**
Source code and deliverables transfer to the client on receipt of final payment. Third-party libraries, fonts and assets stay under their own licences. Client owns and provides the accounts for hosting, domains and third-party services.

**11. Cancellation and late payment**
Work done to date is billable if the project is cancelled; the advance is non-refundable once work starts. Late payments pause work and move delivery dates.

**12. Confidentiality and governing law**
Both parties keep project information confidential. Governing law: [jurisdiction].

**13. Acceptance of quotation**
Reply "Accepted" or sign below and pay the advance to begin.

---
Before sending, check: no internal numbers appear, every item is traceable to the agreed scope, and dates are real.
